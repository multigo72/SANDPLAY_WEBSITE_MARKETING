Privacy Policy

Version 2.0  |  Last Updated: September 2026

Applicable to: SandPlay Mobile Application (iOS) and www.sand-play.com

1. Introduction

SandPlay (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the SandPlay mobile application (“the App”) and supporting website at www.sand-play.com.

This Privacy Notice has been prepared in accordance with:

  • The UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018
  • The EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) 2016/679
  • The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)
  • Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
  • Applicable US state privacy laws

We act as the Data Controller in respect of personal data collected through the App and website. You can contact us regarding privacy matters at support@sand-play.com.

2. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect limited personal data to provide and improve the App. We do not collect names, email addresses, or any direct identifiers. The data we collect is as follows:

2.1 Data Collected Automatically

  • Approximate city-level location (not precise GPS)
  • Device information: model, operating system version, RAM, and app version
  • App usage data: screens viewed, interactions, features used, and interaction behavior
  • Push notification device token (device-specific identifier issued by the operating system)
  • Crash data logs and technical diagnostic information

2.2 Data You Provide Voluntarily

  • Survey responses: optional feedback submitted within the App

2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or direct personal identifiers
  • No IP address tracking
  • No account registration or login credentials
  • No payment or financial information
  • No messaging or social interaction data
  • No special category or sensitive personal data

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes, relying on Legitimate Interests as our lawful basis under UK/EU GDPR Article 6(1)(f) in each case:

  • App Functionality – to provide and operate the SandPlay App and its features
  • Analytics – to understand how users engage with the App and improve the experience
  • Product Improvement – to analyze survey feedback and usage patterns
  • Technical Support – to diagnose and resolve crashes and technical issues

We have carried out a Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) for each of the above purposes and are satisfied that our interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms, given the limited and non-sensitive nature of the data processed.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data for a maximum period of six (6) years from the date of collection, unless a shorter period is required by applicable law or you exercise your right to erasure.

This retention period reflects standard legal limitation periods applicable in the US, UK, and EU, and ensures we can fulfill any legal or regulatory obligations that may arise.

A formal Data Retention Policy will be implemented as part of our ongoing compliance program. Upon expiry of the retention period, personal data will be securely deleted or anonymized.

5. International Data Transfers

SandPlay is operated from the United States. Personal data collected through the App may be stored and processed in the United States through third-party cloud and analytics service providers.

If you are accessing the App from the United Kingdom, the European Union, Canada, or any other jurisdiction outside the United States, please be aware that your personal data may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

We ensure that such transfers are carried out in compliance with applicable law:

  • EU Users: Data transfers are carried out using appropriate safeguards recognized under the EU GDPR, which may include adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms, as applicable.
  • UK Users: Transfers are made in accordance with the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or equivalent adequacy mechanisms recognized under UK GDPR.
  • Canadian Users: Transfers comply with the requirements of PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

By using the App, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to and stored or processed in the United States as described in this Notice.

6. Third Parties and Data Sharing

We share limited personal data with third-party service providers who assist us in operating, analyzing, and improving the App, including:

  • Vercel – cloud hosting, website hosting, infrastructure, and related technical services.
  • PostHog Analytics – product analytics and usage measurement to help us understand how users interact with the App and improve the user experience.

These providers process information on our behalf and are subject to applicable contractual and data protection requirements.

External Websites

When you visit an external website through a link provided within the App or website, the external website may collect information about you independently. We do not control the privacy practices of those third-party websites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes. We do not share personal data with unaffiliated third parties beyond those described above, except where required by law.

We may disclose personal data if required to do so by law, court order, or regulatory authority, or where necessary to protect our legal rights or the safety of others.

7. Cookies and Tracking

The SandPlay iOS mobile application does not use browser cookies.

The SandPlay website (www.sand-play.com) may use cookies or similar technologies when accessed through a web browser. A separate Cookie Policy governing the website may be published as appropriate.

When you access external websites through links from the App or SandPlay website, those websites may use cookies or similar tracking technologies independently of us.

8. Push Notifications

We may offer optional push notifications to provide information about SandPlay features, updates, or other relevant content.

To send push notifications, we use a push notification service that relies on a device token issued by your device’s operating system.

This token does not, by itself, reveal your identity or contact information. You may enable or disable push notifications at any time through your device settings or within the App settings.

Withdrawing permission for push notifications will not affect the lawfulness of any prior processing.

9. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

9.1 Rights Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR

  • Right of Access – to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification – to request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”) – to request deletion of your data
  • Right to Restriction – to request we limit how we use your data
  • Right to Object – to object to processing based on Legitimate Interests
  • Right to Data Portability – to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint – with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at www.ico.org.uk, or your relevant EU supervisory authority

9.2 Rights Under CCPA / CPRA (California Residents)

  • Right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

9.3 Rights Under PIPEDA (Canadian Residents)

  • Right to access your personal information
  • Right to request correction of inaccurate information
  • Right to withdraw consent for data processing where consent is the applicable legal basis
  • Right to file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise any of your rights, please contact us at support@sand-play.com.

We will respond to requests within 30 days, or within any shorter period required by applicable law. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. These include:

  • Encryption of data in transit via HTTPS
  • Secure cloud-hosted environments with restricted access controls
  • No sensitive personal data stored within the App code
  • Server-side processing where applicable
  • Restricted database and infrastructure access limited to authorized personnel

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and, where required, affected individuals, in accordance with our legal obligations.

11. Children’s Privacy

The SandPlay App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@sand-play.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete the information.

We will notify you of material changes by updating the “Last Updated” date at the top of this Notice. We encourage you to review this Notice periodically.

Your continued use of the App following the posting of an updated Notice constitutes your acknowledgement of the changes.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Notice or our data processing activities, please contact:
